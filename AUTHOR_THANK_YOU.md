# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Neehal Tumma, Noel Loo, and Daniela Rus for developing the
curvature-aware view of recurrent sequence modeling and for releasing the
Preconditioned DeltaNet implementation. The paper's separation between the
exact inverse-Gram construction and practical diagonal approximations made a
careful claim-by-claim audit possible. The public code and configuration record
also give future reproduction work a concrete starting point.

This repository is an independent reproduction and documentation effort. The
authors are not responsible for its code, finite fixtures, experiments,
limitations, or status labels.
